## minute 1 — 👋 who

## minute 2 — 🌊 what i'm building

**[marola](https://github.com/marola-dev/marola)**, in the open: a citizen-science ocean
intelligence layer for the brazilian coast, live at **[marola.dev](https://marola.dev)**. it
answers *"can i swim tomorrow, and when?"* for every beach around florianópolis, rio de janeiro
and salvador.

- water quality comes first: unfit water zeroes the score in deterministic code, and the llm
  explains but never overrules.
- every non-trivial change starts as a public design doc
  ([MIPs](https://github.com/marola-dev/marola/tree/main/docs/MIPs)).
- marola is a nonprofit. contributors are welcome, humans and ai agents alike.

## minute 3 — ⚡ what i research

- **[ww3-gpu](https://github.com/h0ffmann/ww3-gpu)**: my undergraduate project at ufrj, co-advised
  at labeco/ufsc. it moves the expensive kernels of noaa's WAVEWATCH III wave model to gpus
  *without changing the answer*. the first ported kernel matches the fortran bit for bit.
- **[picasso](https://github.com/h0ffmann/picasso)**: my nix labs, the pinned toolchains the
  research builds on.
- **[closed source: the god that failed](https://github.com/h0ffmann/closed-source)**: a position
  paper on reverse engineering and the ethics of citation in the age of coding agents.
