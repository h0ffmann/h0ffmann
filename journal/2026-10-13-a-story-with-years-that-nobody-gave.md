# A story with years that nobody gave

## Challenge

The owner asked for a timeline of his path, with the logos of where he worked. He gave the
order and no years.

## Context

A timeline without years is a list, and a timeline with invented years is a lie that looks like
a fact. The README of the day listed the employers with no dates.

## Options

- Ask the owner for each year, and wait.
- Leave the years out.
- Look for them in what he had already written.

## Decision

The third. The README has a history, and one of its commits (20 September 2026) listed each job
with its years, in his words. A helper read every commit that changed the file and returned the
lines with their commit hashes.

## Result

Each mark of the arc names its source: a commit of this README, the record of a DOI, or the
owner's word with its date. Three things he named are not on the arc, because no file gave them:
New York, the word "backend" as a job title, and the years of his last employer. The pull
request said so, and so does the note on the arc.

## Lessons

- A person's own files are the first source about that person. Read the history, not only the
  last version.
- When a fact has no source, leave the place empty and say so. Do not fill it with what is
  likely.
