### people who read

### open source platform

## hiring

### no live coding

i don't do live coding. i'm open to a code interview, or to discussing past projects: my open source ones, and a few closed source ones.

### money first

i won't do a single interview without talking money first.

## time and place

### async, remote first

async and remote first.

### 4h max overlap

a maximum of 4h of overlap or forced sync per day, whether the team is in brazil, the uk, the us, japan or anywhere else.
