# 🤝 ways of working

how i work, one item per heading so each can be shared on its own: every heading below is a link.

## collaboration

### people who read

i only work with people who know how to read: the docs, the issue, the thread, before asking.

### no code reviews

i don't do code reviews. i'm open to discussing my open source projects, and a few closed source ones.

## hiring

### money first

i won't do a single interview without talking money first.

### open source platform

i manage an open source platform: **[marola.dev](https://marola.dev)** ([github.com/marola-dev](https://github.com/marola-dev)).

## time and place

### async, remote first

async and remote first.

### 4h max overlap

a maximum of 4h of overlap or forced sync per day, whether the team is in brazil, the uk, the us, japan or anywhere else.
