# A logo is never a file here

A logo is a trademark of its owner. The site shows the logos of where the owner worked and of
the projects he works with, and it loads each one from its owner's page or from Wikimedia
Commons. `just logos` fails when one stops answering.

The cost of the rule is known. Two logos that the owner asked for are not on the page: LIOC
serves its logo over plain HTTP, which a secure page refuses, and Oceanman's page gave no file
to a fetch. A row with no logo shows a plain mark. Nobody draws a look-alike.

Each logo sits on a white chip and keeps its own colours; it does not take one of
[[202610132300-four-lights]].
