# An Open Model for Open Work

Date: 2026-11-16
Status: Pending Owner Approval

Transitioning AI-assisted development from closed-source models (Claude) to open-weight Chinese models (Qwen, DeepSeek).
Rationale: Open-source projects maintained in good faith since 2024 should not rely on closed, proprietary inference APIs.
Exceptions: Closed-source personal projects (e.g., Leanpub publications) may utilize these models, but core tooling remains open.
