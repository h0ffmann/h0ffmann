# The works hold each other

The knowledge graph of the site is not a drawing of what feels related. An arrow is a tie that
an index or a file states: the Zenodo record of one work names another as cited, referenced or
required, or a paper lists it in its references.

That is why the owner asks that all his DOIs be cited together: a reader of one work should find
the others. The rule is in `REUSE.md` and at the top of `AGENTS.md`.

The place of each work is set by hand, so the picture does not move between two builds. The
colour of each is one of [[202610132300-four-lights]].
