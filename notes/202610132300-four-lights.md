# Four lights

"Ideas are in green, water related topics are in blue, and ouroboric ideas in yellow. Closed
source ideas in red." Then: "Ouroboric ideas, water intuition, green knowledge." Then he asked
for "the ouroboric orange", so the Ouroboric light is orange and no yellow is left.

A colour says what kind of thing a work is, and it says the same on every part of the site.
There are four, and no fifth: what fits none of them is neutral.
