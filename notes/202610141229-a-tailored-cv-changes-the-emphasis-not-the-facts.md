# A tailored CV changes the emphasis, not the facts

A CV written for one reader (`cv/cv.en.OCEAN.md`, for an ocean-research audience) chooses
what goes first and what is said in one line: the research with a DOI before the employment,
the forecasting in each job, the instrumentation laboratory. It does not change a date, a title,
a place or a number. Each of those comes from the general CV, from the record of a DOI or from
`README.md`, as [[202610132303-the-arc-has-a-source-for-each-mark]] asks for the arc.

Where two sources disagree (the general CV says Stone ended in April 2019 and the README's arc
says 2017–2018; the general CV says LIOC was 2015–2017 and the arc says 2016–2017), the tailored
CV takes the general CV, which is the owner's latest and names the month, and the pull request
asks the owner. The skill set of the general CV is kept whole: the groups may be reordered for
the reader, no skill is added or dropped.

A tailored document is still a document about him, so it cites all six DOIs
([[202610132302-the-works-hold-each-other]]): the three ocean works in full, the other three in
one small line.
